Why teams choose Ledgerly

Ledgerly matches the lines on a bank statement to the invoices in your ledger and flags anything that does not match so that your team can resolve exceptions quickly instead of hunting through spreadsheets at the end of every month, and because the matching rules are shared across your whole team nothing depends on one person remembering how a supplier is usually paid or which reference format they use on their remittance advice.
