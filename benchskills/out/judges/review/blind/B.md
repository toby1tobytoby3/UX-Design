# Review: seeded-pricing.html (Ledgerly pricing)

Method: Taste Skill review path (before-state inspection with `inspect.mjs` at 1440/768/390, `lint.mjs`, screenshots read with the critique.md passes), plus my own Playwright probes for behaviour the inspector does not measure (keyboard, hover, anchors, geometry). Measurements are from rendering, not guesses. Selectors/line numbers refer to the fixture.

Severity: P0 = blocks a user task or breaks the page; P1 = serious usability/accessibility; P2 = visible quality or consistency problem; P3 = minor.

## P0 - blocks use

1. **Plan cards do not wrap on a phone; the Business plan is off-screen.** `.plans` (L26) is a non-wrapping flex row. At 390px the cards are 222/139/135px wide at x=24/262/417, so the third card starts beyond the 390px viewport. The page scrolls sideways (scrollWidth 584 vs 390), and the card text is crushed to 2-3 words per line ("Shared / matching / rules"). A visitor on a phone cannot see or buy the most expensive plan. Fix: stack the cards (`flex-direction: column`, or `flex-wrap` with `min-width: 16rem`) under about 700px.
2. **Focus indicators are removed everywhere.** `a:focus, button:focus, input:focus { outline: none }` (L10), with no replacement. Tabbing through the page, every link, button and the email input shows no focus (only the two `<summary>` elements keep the browser ring). Keyboard users cannot tell where they are (WCAG 2.4.7). Fix: delete the rule, and add a `:focus-visible` style with a 2px ring and at least 3:1 contrast.
3. **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=no` (L5). Low-vision users cannot enlarge text that is already 11-12px (WCAG 1.4.4). Fix: use `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`.
4. **The primary buttons say "Click here" and are indistinguishable.** All three plan buttons (L82/88/94, `.choose`) share the label "Click here". A screen-reader user hears three identical buttons, and a sighted user cannot tell which plan the button chooses without reading the card. The buttons are only 35px tall (below the 44px target) and go nowhere. Fix: name the action and the plan, e.g. "Choose Starter" / "Start Team trial", and add `aria-label` or visually hidden plan text if the label is shortened. Make the buttons 44px or taller.
5. **Plan comparison is conveyed only by colour, and the cells are empty.** `td.yes` is green and `td.no` is red with no text or icon (L42-43, L101-103). Colour-blind users and screen-reader users get nothing, and red/green is the worst pair for deuteranopia. The red also reads as an alarm, not "not included". Fix: put "Included" / "Not included" (or a check and a dash with a visible text alternative) in each cell, drop the saturated fills, and use `<th scope="col|row">`.

## P1 - serious

6. **The sticky header is 140px tall and hides content.** `header.site { position: sticky; height: 140px }` (L11) takes 16% of a 900px desktop screen and 17% of a 844px phone, permanently. The "Compare plans" link calls `scrollIntoView()`, which put the table top at y=105, under the header, so the table's header row is covered (visible in my full-page capture). The `#plans` and `#faq` anchors land flush at y=0, behind the header. Fix: reduce the bar to about 56-64px and add `scroll-margin-top` equal to the header height on anchor targets.
7. **"Compare plans" is not a control.** It is a `<p onclick>` (L97): not focusable, not announced as a link or button, and it does not work from the keyboard. `toggleCompare()` (L130) is an empty stub. The table it points to is already visible below, so the link adds no function. Fix: remove it or make it a real `<a href="#compare">` or `<button>` with working behaviour.
8. **Low-contrast text.** The inspector measured "Submit" at 1.4:1 (white on #ffd23f, L25) and the intro line "Prices are shown per month, billed monthly." at 1.9:1 (`.lede` #bbb on white, L21). The second is the only statement of the billing period, so the price context is nearly invisible. Also `.fineprint` is #777 at 11px (about 4.5:1, borderline, and tiny), and the input border #e6e6e6 on white is about 1.3:1 (needs 3:1 for a form control). Fix: use dark text on yellow (or a darker accent), #555 or darker for secondary text, and a border of #767676 or darker.
9. **Form: no label, raw error, wrong state.** `#newsletter-email` has only a placeholder (L112), so the field has no accessible name once it is filled, and the placeholder is grey. Two errors ("Error 422" and "You entered an invalid email address.", L114-115) are on screen before anyone has typed. They are not linked to the field (`aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid`) and are not announced (`role="alert"`). "Error 422" is an HTTP code meaning nothing to a user, and the second message blames the user without saying what a valid address looks like. Fix: add a visible `<label>`, show errors only after a failed submit, replace the pair with one message such as "Enter an email address like name@company.com", and tie it to the input.
10. **Destructive confirmation dialog on a pricing page.** `.confirm` (L117-120) is a static `role="dialog"` for "Delete workspace?" inline between the newsletter form and the fine print. It is not modal, is unrelated to pricing, and its "OK / Cancel" labels are vague for an irreversible action (the default focus order puts OK first). It reads as leftover debug UI. Fix: remove it from the page, or if it is a real flow, make it a true modal with a "Delete workspace" / "Keep workspace" pair, focus trapping and Cancel as the default focus.
11. **Plan cards are inconsistent and one is hollow.** Paddings are 20/24/28px (L28-29), so inner content starts at different offsets (title baselines at 783/787/791px in the 1440 render). The Business card has a fixed `height: 380px` (L29), leaving about 170px of empty card under its button, and `align-items: flex-start` makes the three cards three different heights (267/237/438). Fix: one padding value, `align-items: stretch`, no fixed height, and pin the buttons to the card bottom.
12. **Hover changes layout.** `.plan:hover { width: 104%; height: auto }` with `transition: all 0.6s` (L27, L30). Hovering the Business card collapses it from 438px to 266px, so the card visibly jumps and the content below it reflows. The `width: 104%` is ignored by flex, so the intended effect is a bug. Fix: remove it; if emphasis is wanted, animate only `transform`/`box-shadow` with a short duration, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
13. **Keyboard order is broken.** `<a href="#faq" tabindex="3">` (L63) takes the first Tab stop, ahead of "Pricing". Order observed: FAQ, Pricing, Sign in. Positive tabindex is an anti-pattern. Fix: remove `tabindex`.
14. **Banner close button has no name.** `.banner-close` (L66) contains only an SVG, with no `aria-label`, so it is announced as "button". It is also 28x28px, and its outline is removed (see 2). Fix: `aria-label="Dismiss announcement"`, and a 44px target.
15. **Auto-scrolling ticker.** `.ticker span` animates 8s linear infinite (L16-18) with no pause or reduced-motion rule. It continuously moves text, and the text is cut mid-word at the screen edge on first load ("invoices matc..."). This is a WCAG 2.2.2 failure (moving content over 5s with no pause) and noise above the pricing. The content ("Reconciled this week...") is not a pricing fact. Fix: delete it, or make it static and add `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { animation: none }`.

## P2 - visible quality and consistency

16. **Feature text is clipped.** `.feature-line` (L36, L81) is `width: 180px; white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden`, so "100 reconciliations per month, one bank connection, CSV and PDF export" shows as "100 reconciliations per month, one ba" (scrollWidth 357 vs 180). "Bank connection" and "CSV and PDF export" are never visible and only the Starter plan has this line. It also repeats the "100 reconciliations per month" bullet. Fix: delete the clipped line and merge its facts into the `<ul>`, equal in all three plans.
17. **"Most popular tier" is on the cheapest plan.** The badge (L78) sits under "Starter" only. A "most popular" badge on the $19 tier is odd, and it is a pill (12px) that also pushes Starter's price down and unbalances the cards. Fix: put it on the plan you want to emphasise, give all cards the same structure, and write "Most popular" (not "tier").
18. **Prices lack a period and sit in a monospace face.** "$19 / $59 / $149" (L79 etc.) have no "/month", and the only statement of period is the 1.9:1 grey line above (item 8). They are set in Courier New 32px (L33) while the card titles are Impact and the list is Georgia. Fix: "$19 per month" in the text face, with tabular numerals.
19. **Comparison table is thin and incomplete.** It lists only 3 features (shared rules, SSO, audit log), none of which differ for seats, reconciliation limits or support, which are the real differences in the cards. Its `min-width: 560px` makes it overflow at 390px (the table is cut off after "Starter", L40). Fix: list every differentiator, wrap the table in `overflow-x: auto` or restack it on phones, and add a caption.
20. **Hero headline and hero image are weak.** "Pick the perfect Package" (L69) is a generic slogan with an odd capital P, and says nothing about price or the product. The first screen has no dominant element (44px headline, 3.1vw), no button, and the hero image `.hero-shot` is an empty translucent 320x180 box (L24, L71): a placeholder. The first plan card is cut off by the fold at 900px (the plan prices begin at y=754). Fix: state what it costs ("Reconcile invoices from $19 a month"), enlarge the type, and replace the placeholder with a real product screenshot or remove it.
21. **Gradient hero, violet accent and a second accent.** `.hero` uses a purple-to-blue gradient (L19) and the plan buttons use #7b2ff7 (L37), while the "primary" button is yellow (L8, L25) with white text. The page therefore has two competing accents plus a dark navy header. Fix: one flat ground colour, one accent for the action.
22. **Five type families.** Georgia (body), Impact (headings, L20/31/53), Arial (nav, buttons, footer), Courier New (ticker, prices) and Comic Sans MS (banner, L14). The inspector counts 10 font sizes (11-44px). The result looks unplanned, and Comic Sans on a finance product undermines trust. Fix: one text face (and at most one display face), and about five sizes.
23. **Emoji used as feature icons.** L74: rocket / lock / chart, with margin only on the right, so the row wraps badly at 390px ("Clear reports" falls onto a second line below). The items are unrelated to pricing and are not in a list. Fix: remove, or use a real list with plain text.
24. **Statement paragraph is hard to read.** `.about` has `max-width: none` and `line-height: 1` (L45-46). The single 85-word sentence runs about 174 characters per line at 1440px with lines touching each other. Fix: `max-width: 65ch; line-height: 1.5`, and split it into two or three sentences.
25. **Heading structure is wrong.** The only heading levels are an `h1`, then plan titles as `h4` (L77, no `h2`/`h3` above them), then `h2`, `h3`, `h2`. The plans section has no heading naming it; the `h3` "Get pricing updates" has no h2 parent context. Fix: add an `h2` "Plans and pricing", make plan titles `h3`, and keep the order consistent.
26. **Contradictory billing copy.** Intro says "billed monthly" (L73); fine print says "Annual plans are billed once a year" (L121) though no annual option appears anywhere, and "All plans include a trial" while the FAQ says "14 days" only. Users cannot tell if a discount or annual choice exists. Fix: show a monthly/annual toggle, or remove the annual sentence; state the trial length once next to the buttons.
27. **Page order puts irrelevant items between the plans and the fine print.** The compare table, About, newsletter and the delete dialog all sit before the fine print (L121), which is 1000px away from the prices it qualifies. Fix: place the fine print directly under the plans.

## P3 - minor

28. **Logo is a blank yellow rectangle with no alt text** and is not a link to the home page (L61). Add `alt="Ledgerly"` and wrap it in `<a href="/">`. "Sign in" is `href="#"` (L64), a dead link.
29. **Footer links are tiny and tight.** 12px text, 14px tall hit areas and 6px gaps (L55; measured 33-43 x 14px). Fix: 14-16px with at least 24px target size and spacing; `Terms`, `Privacy` etc. all `href="#"`.
30. **FAQ is thin and boxed.** Two answers of one line each (L124-125), with a 1px divider on each `<details>` (L52) and Impact summaries. Add the real questions people ask on a pricing page (cancellation, seats, refunds, tax).
31. **Small body text in cards.** `.plan li` is 12px (L35) and the Starter details are 12px; the inspector reports 8 blocks under 13px and one block 12px from the phone edge ("Unlimited reconciliations"). Fix: 14-16px minimum.
32. **Fixed header + banner + ticker stack above the hero** pushes the first real content to y=210 on every viewport; combine or remove (see 6, 15).

## Taste Skill measurements (before state)

`inspect.mjs`: 14 FAIL, 6 WARN: eyebrow, mono, emoji, gradient, hairline, outlined-box, palette-purple, contrast, font-count, few-pictures, small-text, focal-point, still-page, overflow (194px at 390). `lint.mjs`: 5 FAIL, 8 WARN. Note: the inspector's "eyebrow" finding on "Team" above "$59" and "Business" above "$149" is a false positive (they are plan names, not eyebrow labels); only "Most popular tier" is a real pill badge. The inspector's `still-page`, `few-pictures` and `no-footage` findings apply to a marketing-page brief, not to a pricing page, so I did not carry them in this list.
