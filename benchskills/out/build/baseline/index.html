Questions, answered

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time; the change applies from your next billing date.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and no card is needed to begin.

What counts as a reconciliation?

One bank statement matched against your ledger for one account in one month.

How does annual billing work?

You are charged once a year at 20% less than twelve monthly payments.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Ask within 30 days of your first charge and we refund it in full.
