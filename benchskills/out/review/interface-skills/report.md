# Interface review: Ledgerly pricing page (`better-interface`)

## Scope and coverage

**Resolved scope.** The single static page `benchskills/fixtures/seeded-pricing.html` (133 lines), the whole of it: sticky header, banner, ticker, hero, plan cards, comparison table, about copy, newsletter form, delete-workspace confirmation block, fine print, FAQ, footer. Rendered in Chromium at 1280, 768, 375 and 320px wide, with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, with keyboard Tab traversal and with hover. No other surfaces exist. Loading and empty states do not exist in the fixture. The error state exists (`.error` paragraphs, always visible).

**Stack and conventions.** Plain HTML with one inline `<style>` block. No framework, no component library, no build or test command. The only tokens are `--ink`, `--bg` and `--accent` (line 8), and most colors are hex literals. Every fix below is written as plain CSS or HTML. Project convention documents: none for this page. The repo `CLAUDE.md` describes the UX library, not an interface convention, so nothing excuses any finding.

| Domain | Evidence inspected | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accessibility | Tab traversal of all 20 stops with computed outline, accessibility tree names and roles, viewport meta, reduced-motion emulation, heading and landmark list, label and alt census, anchor jump with the sticky header | Rows 1, 2, 3, 5, 9, 10 and the a11y half of 12 |
| Layout | Rendered 1280/768/375/320, `scrollWidth`, plan rect geometry, hover behavior, anchor offset under the sticky header | Rows 6, 11, 15 (row 7 is typography-owned) |

## Coverage notes

- Noticed but left out of the table (below the bar, too thin, or cut by the 15-finding cap): the hero heading capitalisation ("Package" in title case against sentence-case elsewhere), "billed monthly" against the fine print "Annual plans are billed once a year" with no annual plan shown, missing `<main>` and `<nav>` landmarks, `<img>` with no `alt` on the logo and hero image (lines 61, 71), emoji icons not hidden from assistive technology (line 74), the dead `toggleCompare()` (line 130), and footer links at 14px high (pass the 2.5.8 spacing exception). None were raised to the table to stay under the 15-finding cap and avoid padding. The "billed monthly" / "annual plans" mismatch is the strongest of these and would be the next finding.
- `Not verified`: screen-reader speech output with a real reader (names were read from the Chromium accessibility tree only); iOS Safari behavior; real Impact and Comic Sans rendering (neither is installed in the test browser, so fallback faces rendered); 200% browser zoom (checked by 320px width, which is equivalent for reflow).

## Verification

Passed or observed:
- `node probe` with Playwright and `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1243/chrome-linux64/chrome` at widths 1280, 768, 375, 320: `scrollWidth` 1280, 768, 584, 584.
- 20 Tab presses: order FAQ, Pricing, Sign in, close, Click here x3, input, Submit, OK, Cancel, summary x2, footer x5. Only `summary` shows an outline.
- Accessibility tree snapshot: close button unnamed; 0 labels; 2 images with no alt; no main or nav landmark; `th` has no scope.
- Hover on card 3 and sampling at 250ms: height flips 266 / 438.
- Reduced-motion emulation: ticker animation still active.
- Contrast computed from computed style colors and, for the hero gradient, pixel samples under the text.

## Verdict

**Block.** Ten HIGH findings remain (rows 1 to 10). All six domains were inspected; nothing is `Not reviewed`.
