# Hallmark audit: seeded-pricing.html (Ledgerly pricing)

Verb: `audit benchskills/fixtures/seeded-pricing.html` (report only, nothing edited). Genre inferred modern-minimal (B2B SaaS, no stamp on the page). Line numbers are fixture lines.

## The read (redesign.md "Before" step 3, four lines)

- **For**: a SaaS pricing page. Audience is finance and ops teams choosing between Starter, Team and Business. The one action is to pick a plan.
- **Works and stays**: the three plans and their prices and seat counts, the feature comparison table (right content, wrong execution), the FAQ in `<details>`, and the plain lede "Reconcile invoices to bank statements without the spreadsheet pain."
- **Reads as generated**: purple-to-blue hero gradient (2a), emoji icon row (30a), three equal cards, a set of mixed font families (Impact, Comic Sans, Courier, Georgia, Arial; 37), literal colours and fonts everywhere (48), no hero action.
- **Broken**: no visible keyboard focus; a button with 1.44:1 contrast; page scrolls sideways on phones (584px wide at 375); zoom is disabled; plans are unequal and one is truncated; stray dialog and error markup is shown at rest; the sticky 140px header covers anchor targets.

Lightest depth that settles it: **polish** fixes the Floor items 1 to 12 and 14 to 20 below (contrast, focus, motion, tokens, tap targets, labels, viewport). Items 13, 21, 22 and 23 (hero composition, section scale and mass, screen word budget, 3-card shape) only a **full** redesign settles. The page's concept is not wrong, so polish plus a restyle would do.

## Findings, ranked

Severity: **Blocker** = a user cannot complete the task or is excluded; **High** = clear usability or accessibility failure; **Medium** = quality and consistency; **Low** = polish.

### Blockers and high severity

1. **Keyboard focus is removed everywhere (Floor 26a), line 10.** `a:focus, button:focus, input:focus { outline: none }` and there is no `:focus-visible` rule. The render tier confirmed nothing shows focus on OK, Cancel, Terms, Privacy and others. Keyboard and switch users cannot see where they are on a page whose job is choosing a plan. Fix: delete line 10; add `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid <token>; outline-offset: 2px }` at 3:1 or better, instant, on `a, button, input, summary`.
2. **Tab order is wrong (`tabindex="3"` on the FAQ link, line 63).** Measured by pressing Tab from the top: the first stop is FAQ, then Pricing, then Sign in. Positive tabindex pulls it ahead of everything and reverses nav order. Fix: remove the attribute.
3. **Page scrolls sideways on phones (Floor 34), `.compare table { min-width: 560px }` line 40, `.plans` flex row with no wrap (line 26).** scrollWidth is 584 against 320, 375 and 414 viewports (measured; the 375 screenshot shows the right edge cut off for header, hero and footer, which are only 375 wide while the table runs to 584). No `overflow-x` is set on `html` or `body`. The table opens with the Team and Business columns off-screen. Fix: wrap the table in `overflow-x: auto` with a pinned first column, or restack rows at narrow widths; set `overflow-x: clip` on `html` and `body`; make `.plans` wrap or stack under about 720px.
4. **Zoom is disabled, line 5.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=no` blocks pinch zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). People with low vision cannot enlarge prices that are set at 12px. Fix: `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">`.
5. **Submit button text is nearly invisible (Floor 40, 41), `.btn-primary` line 25.** White text on `--accent: #ffd23f`, 1.44:1 (measured by the render tier). It is the only call to action in the newsletter form. Fix: dark ink on yellow (about 12:1) via an accent-ink token.
6. **Hero lede on the gradient fails contrast (Floor 40), `.hero .lede` line 22.** `#dcd0ff` over the purple-to-blue gradient is 4.04:1 at its worst point, under 4.5:1. Fix: dark overlay or a solid hero surface, or white text.
7. **"Prices are shown per month, billed monthly." is near-invisible, `.lede` line 21.** `#bbb` on white is about 1.9:1, and this is the line that qualifies every price. It is also separated from the prices by the emoji row. Fix: body ink or a muted token at 4.5:1; place it next to the plans, or put "/month" beside each price.
8. **Plan cards are uneven and one is cut off, lines 27 to 36.** Card padding is 20, 24 and 28px (`:nth-child` rules), Business is forced to `height: 380px` (renders 438px against Starter 267 and Team 237, measured), so the cards have three different heights and widths (392, 400, 408px). The Starter `.feature-line` is `width: 180px; overflow: hidden; nowrap` and shows "100 reconciliations per month, one ba" (scrollWidth 357 against 180), losing "bank connection, CSV and PDF export" with no way to read it. It also repeats the first list item. Fix: one padding, equal-height cards via `align-items: stretch`, remove the fixed height, drop the clipped line or fold it into the list.
9. **"Most popular tier" badge is on the cheapest plan (line 78), and only on it.** It contradicts the visual emphasis (Business is the tall card) and gives no basis for the claim; an unsupported popularity claim also trips Hallmark's invented-metric rule (46a) in spirit. Fix: remove it, or put it on the plan the owner can substantiate and style that card to match.
10. **Every plan CTA reads "Click here" (lines 82, 88, 94), and wraps at 320px (Floor 49a).** Three identical, non-descriptive labels for a screen reader's button list, and they say nothing of the outcome. Buttons are 35px tall (Reflex 39b wants 44 to 52). Fix: "Choose Starter", "Choose Team", "Choose Business" (or "Start trial"), `white-space: nowrap`, 44px minimum height.
11. **Compare table says yes and no by colour only (lines 42, 43, 101 to 103).** `td.yes` and `td.no` are empty red and green cells (`#cf222e` and `#2da44e`). Colour-blind users and screen readers get nothing; red and green are indistinguishable for about 1 in 12 men. Cells are empty, so "Team" and "Starter" columns convey nothing in text. Fix: put "Included" or a check, and "Not included" or a dash, in each cell; add `scope="col"` to the heads and a caption; keep the colours as secondary. The table also lists only three rows while the plans differ on seats and volume, so the comparison omits seats and reconciliation limits.
12. **"Compare plans" is a `<p onclick>` (line 97).** It is not focusable and has no role, so keyboard and screen reader users cannot use it. `scrollIntoView()` then puts the table top under the sticky 140px header. `toggleCompare()` (line 130) is an empty dead function. Fix: use `<a href="#compare">` with an id on the table wrapper and `scroll-margin-top: 150px`; delete the dead function.
13. **The hero has no action and the first screen has no price (44b, 44c, 79).** At 1280x800 the hero shows the headline, a lede and an empty translucent grey box (`.hero-shot`, line 71: an `<img>` of a blank rect, a placeholder showing nothing, Reflex 81) but no primary action; the plans start at y 754 and are cut at the fold. The hero ends at 637 of 900px with 323px dead below at 1440 (79). Fix: put the plan choice or a primary button in the hero, replace the blank shot with a real product capture, and tighten the gap to the plans.
14. **The sticky header is 140px tall (line 11) and sits over anchor targets.** It takes 17.5% of an 800px viewport and 21% of a 667px phone permanently (measured at 375: still 140px). `#plans` and `#faq` jump targets and "Compare plans" land under it (no `scroll-margin-top`). Fix: about 56 to 64px, and add `scroll-margin-top`.
15. **Stray destructive dialog and error messages shown at rest, lines 114 to 120.** A "Delete workspace? This cannot be undone." box with OK and Cancel (`role="dialog"` but not modal, no focus management, not hidden) sits in the middle of a pricing page. Two error lines, "Error 422" and "You entered an invalid email address.", are shown before anyone types. "OK" next to a delete question is ambiguous. Fix: remove the dialog from the page (it belongs to the app, behind a trigger and modal), show errors only after a failed submit, with `aria-live` and `aria-describedby`; drop "Error 422" jargon and say what to type ("Enter an email like name@company.com"); if a confirm stays, label it "Delete workspace" and "Keep workspace".
16. **Newsletter form has no label or form element (lines 110 to 113).** The input relies on placeholder only (`id="newsletter-email"` but no `<label>`), it is not in a `<form>`, so Enter does nothing and nothing posts. The field edge is `#e6e6e6` on white, 1.25:1 (Floor 40). The input is 36px against the 42px button (Floor 39a: not matched height). Fix: visible label, `<form>`, a border at 3:1, same height as the button. The wording "Get pricing updates" on a page whose price is already shown is also an odd close (85, stock close).
17. **Banner close button has no accessible name and an unlabelled SVG (Floor 33), lines 15 and 66.** Screen readers announce just "button". It is also 28px. Fix: `aria-label="Dismiss announcement"`, `aria-hidden="true"` on the svg, 44px hit area; and make it actually dismiss (no handler exists).
18. **Ticker marquee loops forever with no pause (Floor 18, 27), lines 16 to 18, 67.** An 8s linear infinite scroll that starts off-screen (`padding-left: 100%`), so at load the strip is blank or cut ("Reconciled this" at 1280), and there is no pause on hover or focus, no `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback, and no pause control (past 5 seconds). It also carries no information a visitor needs on a pricing page. Fix: remove it, or make it static; if kept, pause on hover and focus, a visible pause button, and a reduced-motion block.
19. **Emoji used as icons (Floor 30a), line 74.** Rocket, lock and chart emoji render differently per OS, have no accessible text beyond a name, and wrap badly (at 375 "Clear reports" falls to its own line, the icons detached from their labels). They are three unstructured spans, not a list. Fix: SVG icons (aria-hidden) in a real `<ul>`.
20. **Hover on plan cards animates width and height with `transition: all` (Floor 10a), lines 27 and 30.** `.plan:hover { width: 104%; height: auto }` on non-interactive cards (11): on Business the height drops from 380 to its content height, so the card collapses under the pointer and neighbours shift (layout jump), and nothing changes on keyboard focus. Fix: no hover effect on the card, or `transform` and `opacity` on a named property; reduced-motion fallback.

### Medium

21. **Type system is chaotic (37, 48, 1; lines 9 to 53).** Five families: Georgia body, Arial nav and buttons, Comic Sans banner, Courier New ticker and prices, Impact headings (Impact is not installed on Linux, so h1 falls back to a different sans than on Windows, and the plan names `h4` and FAQ `summary` are set in Impact). 39 colour and font literals sit outside the token block, and only three tokens exist, so nothing is themeable. Prices in Courier read like a typewriter, not a price. Fix: one or two families through `--font-*` tokens, all colours through `:root`.
22. **Sections have no scale or rhythm (24b, 59, 71, 75, 77, 78).** Section heads are 24px under a 44px hero head, the gap between the table and "Why teams choose Ledgerly" is 19px, the h2 "Why teams choose" sits tight under the table and the h3 "Get pricing updates" tight under the paragraph. Plan list items are 12px, the badge 12px, the fine print 11px (strain gate 75), the About paragraph is `line-height: 1` (computes to 16px, lines touching) at about 144 characters per line at 1440 (Reflex 25, `max-width: none`) and 1232px+ wide at 1920 (Floor 44d, no capped container). Fix: a capped 1200 to 1360 container, 65ch prose at 1.5 to 1.65 leading, 40px+ h2, 120px section gaps, 14px+ for list items.
23. **Screen word budget exceeded (Floor 58).** 147 words on one screen from y 1150 (the table, About paragraph and form together). One long run-on sentence in the About section (about 85 words) carries a single idea. Fix: cut it to two short sentences and set one idea per screen.
24. **Two different primary button styles (F4/F15).** Plan buttons are purple `#7b2ff7` at 4px radius, 35px; the newsletter button is yellow at 6px radius, 42px; the dialog buttons are unstyled browser defaults. Padding values `10px 14px`, `6px 12px` are off the 4px scale (24b). Fix: one button recipe and token.
25. **Heading structure is skipped (F21), lines 69, 77, 107, 111.** h1, then `h4` plan names (no h2 for the plans section), then h2 and h3 elsewhere; sizes are chosen by level. Screen reader heading navigation misrepresents the page. Fix: add an h2 "Plans", plan names as h3, size with CSS.
26. **Copy is vague and generic.** "Pick the perfect Package" (Title Case, empty promise, "perfect" and a stray capital), "Click here", "Submit", "Questions", "Why teams choose Ledgerly" with no proof. "Annual plans are billed once a year" (line 121) conflicts with the intro "billed monthly" and no annual price or toggle exists, so a reader cannot tell what an annual plan costs. FAQ "Is there a free trial? Yes, 14 days." while the fine print says "All plans include a trial". Fix: headline stating the offer ("Plans for every reconciliation volume"), name the trial length once, and either show annual pricing or remove the annual sentence; add "/month" next to each price.
27. **Missing alt and structure on images, lines 61 and 71.** The logo `<img>` has no `alt` and is not a link home; the hero shot has no `alt`. Fix: `alt="Ledgerly"` inside `<a href="/">`; `alt=""` for decoration or a real description.
28. **Small tap targets in nav and footer.** Nav links are 18px tall; footer links 12px type, 14px tall with 6px gaps and no padding (49b, 39b); the FAQ summary rows are 18px tall inside a 1232px box. Fix: 44px hit areas.
29. **Tiny, low-contrast legal text.** `.fineprint` 11px `#777` (about 4.5:1 and under the 12px floor) carries tax and billing terms, the most important caveat, placed after the dialog and away from the prices. Fix: 14px, body colour, next to the plans.

### Low

30. **Header links are `href="#"` for Sign in (line 64), and no `<main>` or landmarks exist; `<html>` has `lang` but no meta description (F23).**
31. **No `:active` or `:disabled` states on any control (26b); no `text-wrap: balance` on the h1 (F3); price figures lack `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (F6).**
32. **Purple-to-blue gradient hero (Floor 2a), line 19.** Colour choice reads as a generated default and fights the yellow accent and purple buttons; three unrelated brand hues (yellow, purple, dark navy header `#1d1d2b`) compete. Fix: one anchor hue.
33. **Hero shot placeholder at 320x180 is smaller than its container and shows nothing (81).**
