## 2. Verdict

Against the FT Visual Vocabulary's 59 chart types: **38 covered**, **4 covered but hard to find** (the template exists, lookup cannot reach it), **6 gaps to build**, **11 skipped** with a reason (niche or decorative). Three more come from outside that list: the funnel (ECharts' funnel series, Diagram Design's pyramid and funnel), the cohort triangle (a specific use of the FT's XY heatmap), and the standard's own section 7, which names fishbone, swimlane and spectrum scale with no template behind them. The library is strong: the gaps are a generic Sankey, funnel, cohort and cross-tab heatmaps, fishbone, swimlane and the pictogram, plus a few small variants. Full mapping in the appendix.

### Risks and what is needed from the owner

- **The build is not in the repo.** The library, register and schema are generated by `build.py`, `assemble9.py` and `post9.py` on the owner's machine. Route A: the owner adds the new pages through that build (this plan and `charts_draw.py` are the spec). Route B: patch the three files directly with python-pptx and openpyxl in one commit. Route B is faster but risks a PowerPoint repair prompt (MAINTAINING.md warns a misordered chart element is enough), so the owner must open the deck in PowerPoint and run the Accessibility Checker before release.
- **Access.** This session has read-only access to the repo. Building needs push access, ideally to a branch and a pull request.
- **Scope.** Internal brand (Appendix A) only; no client-brand variants.
- **Method sources.** Diagram Design is MIT (credit it in `reference_urls`); the FT list and ECharts/RAWGraphs are references only, nothing copied.
