## What it is
A black top bar: the unsection wordmark, two small tabs (**Sections**, **Templates**), a wide central **Search**
field showing a "/" keyboard-shortcut hint, then **Login** as plain text and **Join for free +** as a white pill,
plus an overflow menu with a red notification dot. A "What's new" card appears bottom-right on first visit.

## Key techniques
- **Search-first**: the widest element in the bar, with a visible keyboard shortcut.
- **One bright action** (the white pill); Login stays as text.
- **Tabs for the two content types** rather than a dropdown menu.
- A small dot on the overflow menu signals news without a banner.

## Accessibility
Announce the "/" shortcut and make sure it doesn't hijack typing in other fields. The notification dot needs a text alternative.

## Weaknesses
The "What's new" popup covers content on first load.
