## What it is
Below the filter bar, a 3-column grid of light section screenshots on black. Each card's caption is a dim type
prefix plus a title ("Feature | For You Data Privacy Feature Section", "Testimonial | Composio Customer Results
Testimonial") with a **heart** to save on the right. A native ad card fits the same format ("Ad | Your AI can code.
Give it taste."). Above the grid, a "Trending this week" carousel ("02 / 03 · Base Blockchain Finance Hero
Section · 89 views this week") and a "Latest" list. Scrolling further hits a soft gate: "Join for free to keep
browsing and save the sections you like."

## Key techniques
- **Type | title captions** carry the taxonomy and search terms.
- **Save on every card** (heart), which motivates sign-up.
- **Soft gate after free browsing**, rather than at the door.
- **Trending + Latest** modules show freshness and popularity above the grid.
- **Native ad** in the same card format, clearly labelled.

## Accessibility
Each card should be a single link with the caption as its name; the heart needs a label ("Save …").

## Weaknesses
At three columns the thumbnails are still small; detail needs a click.
